Review share

Review from

Which modules review questions may come from.

Nothing here is graded and nothing is reported to the instructor. Nobody is expected to work every question; there are far more than anyone needs. Each module is divided into topics, and a topic is done when we check it: ten questions, drawn from ones we have not seen, and a score at or above our mastery level (90% unless we change it in Settings) marks the topic mastered. Two weeks later the site brings it back for review, and every practice set mixes in a few review questions from topics we have already worked. Every multiple-choice answer comes with an explanation of why it is right or why it is wrong. Reading the explanations for the options we did not choose is most of the value.

Progress is kept in this browser only, so a different computer or a private window starts fresh.
